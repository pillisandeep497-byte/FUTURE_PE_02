The strategy in four lines

Recognition beats persuasion.

1Lead with what they already feel

Not the brand. Not the offer. "If your hair takes 40 minutes to dry, this is for you." The first two seconds decide the ad.

2Show the process, not the promise

The strand check, the 24-hour patch test, the written price. Process is the only proof a competitor can't copy without actually doing it.

3Admit one honest thing per ad

"You'll be in the chair three to four hours. Bring a charger." One admission makes the other 90% believable.

4Ask for something small

Not "book now". "Send a photo — price in ten minutes." Nobody books a 3-hour service from a 20-second video.

Platform cutdowns

One script, six publishable variants.

Instagram Reels

30s full story · 15s problem + proof + CTA · 7s hook + result + CTA. Hook window 1.5s. Burned-in captions, 3–5 words per line, above the bottom 20%.

YouTube Shorts

3s hook window, more information density tolerated. Title: "Why I turned down a ₹6,000 booking at my hair studio" (58 chars). Captions mandatory — high sound-off viewing.

Meta paid ads

3 copy variations with primary text, headline and description within platform limits. No personal-attribute language, no guaranteed outcomes, results-vary on before/after creative.

  • Hook visible on screen within 1.5 seconds — every variant
  • Captions burned in; 85% of social video is watched muted
  • First frame is never a logo or a title card
  • No shot longer than 3 seconds; CTA on screen for the last 3
  • One CTA per video — a micro-ask for cold traffic, never a purchase
  • Trending audio is for organic posts only; paid ads use licensed audio

Quality & compliance

What the QA pass caught before delivery.

Authenticity scan — 11 fixes

"Transform your hair with our premium keratin treatment!" → "Keratin didn't ruin your hair. The shortcut did."

"Say goodbye to bad hair days forever" → "Five months later, I still haven't touched the iron."

"Link in bio to book your appointment" → "Send a photo on WhatsApp — price in ten minutes."

Compliance gate — 2 blockers flagged

Blocked: the review-reaction script needs a real Google review, written permission and a blurred surname before it can be shot.

Blocked: all prices marked (verify) must be confirmed — a wrong price in a paid ad is both a customer-service and an ASCI problem.

Required: "Results vary" burned into any before/after creative; #ad for paid creators.

## 1. Identity
- **Brand:** Glow Studio by Sanjana
- **Category:** Local service (beauty salon & bridal studio)
- **Hero offer (ONE):** Hair keratin / smoothening treatment — with bridal packages as the secondary campaign
- **Price:** Keratin from ₹4,500 (usually ₹4,500–₹8,000); smoothening from ₹3,800; bridal from ₹18,000; pre-bridal ₹15,000–₹25,000 *(verify)*
- **Location:** 1st Floor, above Sri Sai Bakery, MVP Colony Main Road, Sector 4, Visakhapatnam — 530017
- **Hours:** 10:00 AM – 8:30 PM, all seven days

## 2. Customer
- **Primary customer:** Women aged 24–40 in Vizag. Working professionals who wash their hair at 6:30 AM and leave with it still damp, and brides planning a wedding 3–12 months out.
- **Their day:** Up at 6, 40 minutes fighting frizz with a dryer and an iron. Coastal humidity wins by 10 AM. They've stopped expecting a good hair day.
- **What they want emotionally:** To stop thinking about their hair. To walk into an office or a wedding and not worry about how it looks in photos.

## 3. Problem & alternatives
- **Problem in their words:** "I spend 40 minutes on my hair every morning and it still looks frizzy by lunch." *(from review research; confirm with real customers)*
- **What they tried before (ranked):**
  1. Chain salon keratin — cheaper, but the result lasted three weeks
  2. DIY keratin/straightening kits from an e-commerce app — damaged hair, wasted ₹1,500
  3. Home parlour services — inconsistent, no patch test, no guarantee
  4. Doing nothing, wearing it up every day
- **Cost of the problem:** ~40 minutes a day (240+ hours a year), constant low-grade embarrassment in photos, and money already spent on failed fixes.

## 4. Offer & proof
- **What happens, step by step:** 15-minute strand assessment → clarifying wash → protein treatment section by section (3–4 hours) → heat sealing → finishing trim → written aftercare sheet
- **Timeline:** 3–4 hours in the chair. Result lasts 3–5 months.
- **Strongest proof:**
  - A **24-hour patch test before every colour service** — rare in the area and openly advertised
  - **Senior stylists only** on chemical work; junior team does washes and blow-dries
  - **Written price before the service starts** — no mid-service additions
  - L'Oréal Professionnel / Schwarzkopf / O3+ products
  - 300+ brides, 4,500+ clients, 7 years in the same MVP Colony building *(verify)*
- **3 verbatim customer quotes** *(REPLACE with real Google reviews before publishing — these are format examples)*:
  1. "They were honest that I needed repair first, not keratin. Three months later it's still smoothening easily."
  2. "Nobody tried to sell me anything extra. Price was written down before they started."
  3. "Makeup was still intact at 11 PM in October humidity."

## 5. Objections & limitations
- **Top objection (exact wording):** "Will keratin damage my hair?" / "My friend's hair got ruined after keratin."
- **Other objections:** "It'll be too expensive." · "They'll upsell me." · "How long will it actually last?" · "Will the same person do my wedding-day makeup?"
- **Honest limitations:**
  - Long services take 3–4 hours — clients must plan their day
  - It's not the cheapest in MVP Colony, and it isn't trying to be
  - Keratin isn't suitable for hair bleached twice in three months — the studio will say no
- **Who this is NOT for:** Anyone wanting a 45-minute cheap fix; anyone wanting a completely different hair texture; heavily bleached hair without a repair phase first.

## 6. Competitive position
- **Why customers pick Glow Studio (specific enough that a competitor can't say it):** The studio openly **disqualifies** customers — "if your hair can't take it, we'll say no and suggest a repair plan" — and puts a **patch test and a written price** in front of every chemical service. Chain salons compete on volume; home parlours compete on price; nobody competes on refusing work.

## 7. Campaign
- **Primary CTA:** WhatsApp a hair photo → price range in 10 minutes
- **Secondary CTA:** Walk in weekday 11 AM–4 PM (short services) / book a free 15-minute consultation
- **Real urgency:** Wedding season Nov–Feb; bridal trials limited to 4 per weekend; keratin booked 2 days ahead on weekends. **No fake deadlines.**
- **Platforms:** Instagram Reels (organic) + Meta paid + YouTube Shorts. WhatsApp status for local distribution.
- **Who films:** Sanjana (founder) + 1 senior stylist + 2 real clients with consent
- **Production constraints:** Phone only. Filmed inside the studio during working hours. Clients' faces only with written consent — back-of-head and hands-only shots preferred.

## 8. Voice
- **Sounds like:** honest, experienced, unhurried
- **Never sounds like:** salesy, clinical, discount-driven, fake-luxury
- **Language:** English, with light Telugu warmth in captions/comments only (hero lines stay English)
- **Words the brand hates:** "cheap", "parlour" (they say studio), "pamper", "transformation", "glow-up"

## 9. Compliance red lines
- No "guaranteed results", no "permanent", no medical or dermatological cure claims
- No promise of a specific outcome like "hair will be straight for 5 months" — say "usually lasts 3–5 months"
- Before/after must be real, unretouched, consented, and accompanied by "results vary"
- Paid creator content needs **#ad**
- No "#1 salon in Visakhapatnam" or "best in the city" — unverifiable superlatives
- Testimonials must be real and used with permission; keep proof of the original review
- Music: trending audio is for organic posts only. Paid ads need commercial-licence audio.

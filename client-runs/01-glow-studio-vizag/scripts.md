**On-screen text:** `40 minutes. Every morning.` (0–4s) · `240 hours a year` (5–8s) · `Strand check first` (9–13s) · `3–5 months` (19–23s) · `Photo → price in 10 min` (26–30s)

**Shot list:** 1) Bathroom mirror, drying hair, watch visible · 2) Close-up: damp ends, clock/timer · 3) Studio: fingers testing a strand · 4) Basin wash, water running · 5) Treatment applied section by section · 6) Sealing with heat tool · 7) Aftercare sheet handed to client · 8) Phone showing the WhatsApp reply.

**B-roll:** hair falling through fingers · product bottles on the counter · the chair being wiped down between clients.

**Audio direction:** Original sound — voiceover-only is fine for paid ads, but shooting it as the person's own voice makes it native for Reels. Duck a soft trending track under it.

**Filming notes:** Bathroom scene at home (morning light), studio scenes between 11 AM and 4 PM on a weekday when it's quiet. Two takes maximum per beat. Audience of one — speak to the phone, not a crowd.

> Rationale: The agitation beat uses a *number the viewer already feels* (40 minutes) rather than an adjective. The limitation line at 18–25s is what makes the CTA believable.

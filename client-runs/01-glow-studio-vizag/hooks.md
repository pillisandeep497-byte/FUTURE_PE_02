## Hook engineering notes (why the winners win)

**1. Numbers beat adjectives.** Three of the six winners contain a number (₹12,000, ₹6,000, 40 minutes, three hours). Numbers create specificity, and specificity is the only thing viewers can't get from a competitor.

**2. Confession outperforms claims.** "I spent ₹12,000 before I found this place" implies incompetence on the speaker's part — which is exactly why it's believable. A brand can't say "we're the best", but a customer can say "I was wrong".

**3. Local identity is a free multiplier for local businesses.** "Vizag humidity has already won by 10 AM" works nowhere else in the country. For a business with a 5 km radius, hyper-local hooks beat generic ones on relevance even with lower production value.

**4. The hook must survive with the sound off.** Every promoted hook has an obvious visual partner: a phone timer at 40 minutes, a before/after split, a stack of receipts. Scripts specify these as first frames.

**5. Generate 24, shoot 3.** Hooks are the cheapest variable to test. The same script with three different hooks is three ads for the price of one shoot — this is the single highest-ROI practice in paid short-form.

### Option C — The hook test (most engaging)

Post the 6 top hooks as a single image/carousel and ask followers to vote:
> "I wrote 24 hooks for a Visakhapatnam salon. These are the 6 I'd actually shoot. Which one stops YOUR scroll?"
> `1. "I spent ₹12,000 on keratin before I found this place."`
> `2. "I turn down ₹6,000 bookings. Let me explain why."`
> `3. "Keratin didn't ruin your hair. The shortcut did."`

> `5. "Same hair. Three hours apart."`
> `6. "My friend said don't go. She was wrong."`
> "Comment 1–6 👇 Made for the @future_interns task."

*Why it works:* voting is the lowest-effort comment a person can leave, and comments are what push reach.

## Tagging & logistics (don't skip these)

- **Tag @future_interns** in the **video/carousel itself** (use the "Tag people" option), not only in the caption — a tag in the media is more visible and gets a notification.
- Also tag them in the **first comment**: "Task 2 for @future_interns — UGC ad scripts."
- **Add a location tag:** Visakhapatnam. Local tags pull local reach, and local business owners are exactly who you want to see it.
- **Post between 7–9 PM IST** (highest scroll time in India) or 1 PM.
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour** — engagement in hour one drives the reach for the next three days.
- **Pin your own comment** with the repo link and a line like: "If you run a local business and want a free hook audit, comment 'HOOK' 👇"
- **Stories:** post 2 stories — one screenshot of the hook list, one of the script with a "which hook is best?" poll. Stories convert viewers into commenters.

## What to actually film (if you want the strongest version)

Film **one of the scripts yourself** with a friend or a real local shop, then post it as the Reel with the caption above. Nothing proves a UGC system like a finished UGC ad.

Simplest version: Script 4 (objection-crusher) needs one person, one location, 30 seconds, no client footage. Or record it as a **voiceover over b-roll you shoot in 10 minutes** on any street in MVP Colony.

**If you have no access to the business at all:** post the Reel as "script on screen + your voice reading it". That's still a real UGC creative — it's how agencies pitch before the shoot.

## Do / Don't

**Do**
- Explain the *problem the ad solves*, not just that you wrote scripts. That's the actual task requirement.
- Show the hook list — it's the most impressive artefact you have.
- Credit the brief: "@future_interns task".

**Don't**
- Don't present the salon as a paying client. Say "a studio in Vizag" or "a representative local business" — honesty is the whole point of the pack.
- Don't claim results you don't have ("this got 1M views"). You have scripts, not performance data yet.
- Don't post AI-generated faces as if they were real customers.
